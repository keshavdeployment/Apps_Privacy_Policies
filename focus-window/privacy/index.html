Privacy Policy — Focus Window

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Focus Window ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the app.

We believe your focus time should remain private. Focus Window does not collect personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Focus Window does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media
  • Financial or payment information
  • Device identifiers
  • Personal usage information

2. How We Use Your Information

We do not collect personal information, so we do not use, analyze, sell, or share your personal information.

Focus Window's core functionality, including the Pomodoro timer and visual scenes, can be used without providing personal information.

3. Data Storage

Focus Window does not operate a backend server or database for collecting user information.

We do not maintain user accounts or user profiles.

Any app settings or preferences that may be stored locally on your device remain on your device and are not transmitted to us.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of Focus Window does not use third-party services that collect personal information.

The app currently does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Analytics services
  • Tracking services
  • User authentication services
  • Cloud databases
  • Crash-reporting services that collect personal information
  • Social media tracking services

5. In-App Purchases

The current version of Focus Window does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

The app does not collect or process payment or financial information.

6. Children's Privacy

Focus Window does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the app does not collect personal information, users can use Focus Window without providing personal details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user personal information with third parties because Focus Window does not collect personal information.

8. Data Security

Since Focus Window does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a database of user personal information.

We nevertheless aim to follow reasonable security practices in developing and maintaining the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Focus Window's features or data practices change.

If we introduce features that collect, store, or process user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date shown at the top of this policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Focus Window's privacy practices, please contact us:

Email: velocitynexuslabs@gmail.com
