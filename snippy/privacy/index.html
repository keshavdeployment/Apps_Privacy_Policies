Snippy Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Snippy ("Snippy", "we", "us", or "our") is an intelligent website organization application that helps you save websites and organize them using features such as AI-generated summaries, tags, categorization, semantic search, and Smart Collections.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Snippy collects, how we use it, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

By using Snippy, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Snippy is designed to collect and store only the information needed to provide its core functionality.

1.1 Saved Websites

When you save a website in Snippy, we store information associated with the saved website, which may include:

  • The website URL
  • The website's title or other information required to display your saved item
  • Information generated or stored to organize the saved website, such as summaries, tags, categories, and Smart Collections
  • Information required to identify duplicate saved websites
  • The date and time the website was saved

Your saved websites are stored so that Snippy can provide your personal website library, organization, search, and related features.

1.2 Thumbs-Up Feedback

Snippy may allow users to give a thumbs-up to a website.

We may store aggregated thumbs-up information associated with a website to understand which websites are useful or positively rated.

Thumbs-up information is designed to be stored without identifying the individual user who provided the thumbs-up. We do not use thumbs-up information to create individual advertising profiles or sell such information.

1.3 Country

Snippy may collect your country or region.

We may use country information to understand general geographic usage, support localization, provide an appropriate service experience, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

We do not use this information to determine your precise physical location.

1.4 Information Automatically Processed by Our Services

When you use Snippy, our hosting, analytics, subscription, security, and other infrastructure providers may process limited technical information needed to operate their services. Depending on the provider and configuration, this may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device or operating-system information
  • App version
  • Browser or app-related information
  • Usage events
  • Diagnostic information
  • Subscription or purchase-related identifiers

The specific information processed may vary depending on the platform and services you use.

2. How We Use Information

We use information collected through Snippy to:

  • Store and display websites you save
  • Synchronize your saved library where synchronization is provided
  • Generate summaries, tags, and categories
  • Create and maintain Smart Collections
  • Provide search and organization functionality
  • Detect duplicate saved websites
  • Understand general product usage and improve Snippy
  • Maintain application security and reliability
  • Process and manage subscriptions
  • Respond to support requests
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized use
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your saved website library for targeted advertising.

3. AI Processing

Snippy uses artificial intelligence to provide features including website summaries, automatic tagging, categorization, and organization.

To provide these features, relevant information from a saved website may be processed by OpenAI, our third-party AI service provider.

Depending on the feature, information sent for AI processing may include:

  • Website URL
  • Website title
  • Website description or metadata
  • Relevant publicly available website content
  • Other information reasonably necessary to generate the requested AI result

We send only information reasonably necessary to perform the applicable AI functionality.

OpenAI processes API inputs and outputs under its applicable API data-use policies. OpenAI currently states that data submitted to its API is not used to train or improve its models by default, unless the customer explicitly opts in. (OpenAI)

Snippy does not authorize OpenAI to use your saved website library for advertising.

For additional information about OpenAI's handling of API data, please refer to OpenAI's applicable privacy and data-use documentation.

4. Service Providers and Data Sharing

Snippy does not sell, rent, or trade your information.

We use selected third-party service providers to operate the application. These providers may process information on our behalf or as necessary to provide their services.

Supabase

We use Supabase for database and backend infrastructure.

Saved website information and other information necessary to operate your Snippy account may be stored using Supabase's infrastructure.

Vercel

We use Vercel for application hosting and related infrastructure.

Information may pass through Vercel infrastructure when necessary to provide Snippy's application and backend functionality.

OpenAI

We use OpenAI to provide AI-powered features such as summarization, tagging, and classification. Relevant website information may be transmitted to OpenAI for this purpose.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and entitlement information.

RevenueCat may process information such as an application user identifier, subscription status, purchase history, product information, and information needed to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat states that its service processes purchase history and may process a user ID where an app uses a custom application user identifier. (RevenueCat)

Payment credentials are handled by the applicable app-store or payment provider rather than stored directly by Snippy.

PostHog

We use PostHog for product analytics.

PostHog may process information about how users interact with Snippy, such as feature usage, events, app or device information, and related diagnostic or usage information, depending on our configuration.

Analytics are used to understand product usage, identify problems, measure feature performance, and improve Snippy.

We do not use PostHog analytics to sell your saved website library to advertisers.

5. No Sale of Personal Information

Snippy does not sell your personal information.

In particular, we do not sell:

  • Your saved website URLs
  • Your saved website library
  • Your summaries
  • Your tags
  • Your Smart Collections
  • Your thumbs-up feedback
  • Your country information

We do not provide your saved library to advertisers for targeted advertising.

Our service providers receive information only as reasonably necessary to provide the services described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Website Content

Snippy allows you to save links to third-party websites.

To provide AI-powered organization features, Snippy may retrieve and process publicly accessible information from a website you choose to save.

For example, depending on the website and the feature being provided, Snippy may process publicly available:

  • Page title
  • Meta description
  • Headings
  • Relevant page text
  • Other publicly accessible page information

Snippy does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or availability of information provided by third-party websites.

Snippy is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites that you visit through saved links.

7. Data Storage

Your saved website information is stored using Supabase infrastructure.

Information may also be temporarily processed by Vercel, OpenAI, PostHog, RevenueCat, or other service providers when necessary to provide their respective services.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain your saved website information for as long as necessary to provide Snippy's services or until you delete it, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

When you delete an individual saved website, we will remove it from your active Snippy library, subject to limited retention that may be necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backups, or other legitimate operational purposes.

When you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify associated account information and saved library data, subject to legally permitted or required retention.

Third-party service providers may have their own limited retention periods under their respective policies and service arrangements.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If Snippy provides account creation, you may request deletion of your account and associated data.

Snippy will provide:

  • A method within the app to request account deletion; and
  • An external web-based method for requesting account deletion.

When an account deletion request is completed, Snippy will delete or de-identify the personal data associated with the account, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.
